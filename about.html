About Becky Rose

Becky Stadnick is a Minnesota writer, mother, crafter, rock hound, and lifelong collector of things she doesn’t need.

Her debut memoir, Laughing Baby, Crying Mom: On Intrusive Thoughts, Laughter, and Putting Myself Back Together, explores the messy, funny, frightening, and unexpectedly beautiful reality of motherhood—including intrusive thoughts, anxiety, identity, and the strange experience of becoming someone new while trying to raise tiny humans.

Becky writes about the things mothers don't always say out loud: the thoughts that scare us, the moments that make us laugh when we probably shouldn't, the absurdity of parenting, and the small, ordinary moments that make being a mom worth it.

When she isn't writing, Becky can usually be found spending time with her two kids and their dog, searching for interesting rocks, making a recipe or craft she saw online, or impulsively starting a large project.

She hopes her writing makes readers laugh, feel seen, and—most importantly—feel a little less alone.
