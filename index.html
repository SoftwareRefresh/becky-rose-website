Laughing Baby, CRYING MOM On Intrusive Thoughts, Laughter, and Putting Myself Back Together

Buy the book Read the first words

The beautiful, heartbreaking, often invisible parts.

In this deeply personal memoir, Becky Rose reflects on the beautiful, heartbreaking, and often invisible realities of early motherhood. With unapologetic honesty and disarming humor, Becky invites readers into life shaped by postpartum depression and anxiety-the kind that hums beneath the surface while the world expects you to keep smiling, keep giving, and keep going. As she navigates sleepless nights, intrusive thoughts, crushing guilt, and moments of unexpected joy, she wrestles with the widening gap between the woman she was and the mother she believed she had to become.

“A woman doing her best to show up, night after night, beneath the dim glow of a red nursery light.”

About Becky Rose

Becky Stadnick is a Minnesota writer, mother, crafter, rock hound, and lifelong collector of things she doesn’t need.

Becky writes about the things mothers don't always say out loud: the thoughts that scare us, the moments that make us laugh when we probably shouldn't, the absurdity of parenting, and the small, ordinary moments that make being a mom worth it.
