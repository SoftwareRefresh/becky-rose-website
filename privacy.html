Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Becky Rose. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our site. By accessing and using this website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply to you under applicable laws.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect both personal and non-personal information, depending on how you interact with our site:

Personal Information (Provided by You)

  • Signing up for our newsletter (name, email)
  • Submitting inquiries through our contact form (name, email, message content)
  • Posting a comment on a blog post (name, email, comment content). Your email address is never displayed publicly — it is used only to moderate comments and to generate your avatar. Comments are checked by automated anti-spam validation before they are published.
  • Making a purchase, which may include billing/shipping details and payment info via third-party processors
  • Order details and purchase history
  • Downloading digital products, which may include records of access or delivery

Information Uploaded by this website owner

The site owner may upload contact information to this site, such as email lists or audience data, for the purpose of managing communications and relationships with readers. This information is provided and managed by the site owner.

The site owner represents that any personal information uploaded to this site was collected in compliance with applicable privacy, data protection, and email marketing laws, and that appropriate consent has been obtained where required.

Automatically Collected Information

We use tracking technologies to analyze our website traffic and email engagement in order to improve it. These may include analytics software and cookies to store preferences and session data. This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Personal information collected through this site may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted via the contact form
  • Send newsletters and promotional emails (if subscribed)
  • Manage mailing lists and audience segmentation
  • Deliver digital products and provide access to downloads
  • Process and manage transactions and orders
  • Send transactional communications, such as order confirmations or delivery notifications
  • Analyze user behavior and improve website functionality
  • Process transactions and payments (if applicable in the future)
  • Track affiliate links and commissions (if applicable)
  • Enable embedded video content (YouTube, etc.)

Information processed through this site is handled at the direction of this site owner, who determines how and why personal data is used.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies (site functionality)
  • Analytical cookies
  • Marketing cookies such as affiliate tracking

The Service may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to improve functionality and analytics. Users are responsible for managing browser settings to control these technologies. The service may implement additional consent mechanisms in the future. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data, but we may share it with:

  • Service providers such as, but not limited to, email service providers, analytics tools, payment processors, printing and distribution services
  • Email delivery and communications providers
  • Analytics and performance monitoring tools
  • Shipping or fulfillment providers (for physical products)
  • Advertising & affiliate networks
  • Legal authorities if required by law

These third parties receive only the information necessary to perform their services and are subject to their own privacy practices. We do not sell personal information as defined under applicable privacy laws.

If you buy something on this site, the email address you provide may be shared in an irreversibly encoded ("hashed") form with this site owner's advertising and analytics tools (for example, Google Ads). This lets the site owner see whether their ads led to sales. Your actual email address is never sent for this purpose, and these tools can only match the encoded version to an account they already have for you.

6. eCommerce & Payment Processing

If eCommerce is featured on this site, we will process payments via third-party payment providers (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We do not store credit card details.

Order-related information, such as purchase history, delivery records, and download access, may be stored to support fulfillment, customer service, recordkeeping and marketing. This site owner is responsible for the products offered and their fulfillment.

7. Email Communication

If you subscribe to emails from this site, your information may be used to send newsletters, marketing messages, or other communications from the site owner.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each email. Transactional or service-related emails (such as order confirmations) may still be sent as necessary.

8. Embedded Content (Future Feature)

Some pages may contain embedded videos (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo). These platforms may collect your data as per their own privacy policies.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data
  • Opt-out of marketing emails via the "unsubscribe" link in the emails
  • Manage cookies via your browser settings

To exercise your rights, reach out to us in the Contact form on this website.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information such as names, email addresses, and purchase records only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including managing orders, delivering products, and communicating with subscribers. We do not store credit card information, which is processed securely by third-party payment providers.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted on this page, and significant changes may be communicated via email or a website notice.

13. Contact Information

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the contact form or email address listed on this website.

By using this website, you acknowledge and consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
